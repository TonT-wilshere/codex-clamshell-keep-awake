# Clamshell Awake 1.2.0 / 合盖工作 1.2.0

Keep Codex, Claude Code, terminal scripts, builds, and downloads running when you close your MacBook lid.

让 Codex、Claude Code、终端脚本、构建和下载任务在 MacBook 合盖后继续运行。

## English

### What’s new

- Added a Chinese / English switch in the top-right corner and remembers your last choice.
- Changed automatic low-battery protection from 20% to 10%.
- Automatically lowers the built-in MacBook display to minimum brightness after enabling, then restores the previous brightness when disabled, quit, stopped by battery protection, or recovered on the next launch.
- Localized the menu bar, system dialogs, buttons, and status messages in both Chinese and English.
- Ships as one Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 12 or newer.

### Download

- `.dmg`: recommended for most users.
- `.zip`: portable archive for direct extraction.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums for verifying the downloads.

### First launch and safety

This independent open-source build is not Apple-notarized yet. If macOS blocks the first launch, right-click the app in Finder and choose **Open**, or use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Only enable clamshell work mode on a hard, ventilated surface—never put a running MacBook inside a bag.

## 中文

### 新功能

- 窗口右上角新增中文 / English 一键切换，并记住上次选择。
- 低电量自动保护阈值从 20% 调整为 10%。
- 开启成功后自动将 MacBook 内置屏幕亮度降至最低；关闭、退出、低电量保护或下次启动时恢复原亮度。
- 菜单栏、系统提示、按钮和状态文案完整双语化。
- 一个 Universal 安装包同时支持 Apple Silicon、Intel 和 macOS 12 及更高版本。

### 下载

- `.dmg`：推荐大多数用户安装。
- `.zip`：适合直接解压使用。
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`：用于校验下载文件。

### 首次运行与安全提示

这是未经 Apple 公证的独立开源构建。首次打开若被 macOS 拦截，请在 Finder 中右键应用并选择“打开”，或前往“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 仍要打开”。开启合盖工作时请把 Mac 放在坚硬、通风的表面，切勿放入包中。
