# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-20

- Added an in-app Chinese / English switch with persisted preference.
- Changed the low-battery safety cutoff from 20% to 10%.
- Added built-in display dimming on enable and original-brightness restoration on disable, exit, safety cutoff, or next launch after interruption.
- Added bilingual documentation and clearer lifecycle boundaries.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-08-28

- Added a native control panel and menu-bar controls.
- Fixed reopening the control panel after the red close button hides it.
- Added a custom app icon and Universal Apple Silicon + Intel packaging.
- Added battery safety cutoff, process-bound cleanup, and kernel-state verification.
